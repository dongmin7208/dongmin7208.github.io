English

Moon Rabbit Hop ("the app") is a single-player offline game. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your best score and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data and crash diagnostics, in order to show and measure ads. On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the system prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change it anytime from Settings → Privacy choices. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases. "Remove ads" is processed by Apple or Google. The app never sees your payment details.

Rankings. Rankings use Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android. Only if you sign in, your score and player name are sent to Apple or Google and shown to other players. Everything except rankings works without signing in.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Contact. Questions about this policy: email MORU LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY at info@moru.tech.
